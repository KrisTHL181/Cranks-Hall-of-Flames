认捐 / The Pledge

新公司挂牌那天，CEO说：新公司，新文化，不搞老一套。

The new company launched on a Monday. The CEO said: New company, new culture, no old habits.

第一次捐款，群里乱了。有人捐二十，有人捐五十，有人捐三百。部门经理在群里说：随意就行，量力而行。

The first donation drive, the chat was chaos. Someone gave twenty, someone gave fifty, someone gave three hundred. The department manager said in the group chat: Give whatever you're comfortable with.

大家觉得这家公司确实不一样。

Everyone felt this company was truly different.

二

第二次捐款，邮件标题写着：目标——参与率80%。

The second drive, the email subject line read: Goal — 80% participation.

群里安静了。没有人再问“捐多少”。大家看了看去年的数字，往上加了一点。

The chat went quiet. No one asked "how much." Everyone looked at last year's number and added a little.

普通员工捐五十，资深员工捐一百，主管捐一百五，经理捐二百。

Regular staff gave fifty, senior staff gave a hundred, supervisors gave a hundred and fifty, managers gave two hundred.

没有人商量过。但数字整整齐齐。

No one had discussed it. But the numbers lined up perfectly.

三

第三次，部门经理在群里发了五个字：按上次标准。

The third time, the department manager posted five words in the chat: Same as last time.

底下秒回：“收到。”“收到。”“收到。”

Replies came instantly: "Got it." "Got it." "Got it."

没有人问“上次标准是多少”。每个人都知道自己该站哪一行。

No one asked what the standard was. Everyone knew which row they belonged in.

四

今年来了个新人，小林，刚毕业。

This year, a new hire arrived. Lin, a fresh graduate.

第一次捐款，他在群里发了三百。

His first donation drive. He posted three hundred in the chat.

群里安静了。

The chat went silent.

那三秒钟的安静，比任何一句话都响。

Those three seconds of silence were louder than any words.

部门经理私信他：“你刚来，捐五十就行。”

The department manager messaged him privately: "You just started. Fifty is fine."

小林撤回三百，重发五十。

Lin retracted the three hundred and resent fifty.

群里恢复了正常。

The chat returned to normal.

五

两年后，小林成了老人。又来一个新人，捐了三百。

Two years later, Lin was the old hand. Another new hire arrived. She gave three hundred.

小林盯着屏幕看了很久。

Lin stared at the screen for a long time.

他想起自己第一年的三百块钱，想起那三秒钟的安静，想起经理的私信，想起自己撤回重发时手指有点抖。

He remembered his own three hundred from his first year. The three seconds of silence. The manager's private message. His fingers trembling slightly as he retracted and resent.

他现在是老人了。他知道该怎么做。

He was the old hand now. He knew what to do.

他私信新人：“你刚来，捐五十就行。”

He messaged the new hire: "You just started. Fifty is fine."

新人撤回三百，重发五十。

She retracted the three hundred and resent fifty.

群里没人注意到这件事。

No one in the chat noticed.

六

年底，办公室参与率百分之九十四。HR发了贺信。CEO在全员会上表扬了大家。

At year-end, the office participation rate hit ninety-four percent. HR sent a congratulatory email. The CEO praised everyone at the all-hands.

散会后，小林路过电梯口。

After the meeting, Lin passed by the elevator.

墙上贴着一张纸：“年度慈善捐款——自愿参与。”

A piece of paper was taped to the wall: "Annual Charity Drive — Voluntary Participation."

下面有一行手写的字，墨迹已经淡了：“参照去年标准。”

Below it, a handwritten line, the ink already faded: "Refer to last year's standard."

再下面，又有一行新的字，笔迹很新：“跟去年一样就行。”

Below that, another line in fresh handwriting: "Same as last year is fine."

小林站了一会儿。

Lin stood there for a moment.

他想起自己第一年看到这张纸的时候，问过自己一个问题：如果没有人规定标准，我会捐多少？

He remembered seeing this paper his first year and asking himself a question: If no one set a standard, how much would I give?

他现在已经不记得那个答案了。

He could no longer remember the answer.

他推开门，走进风里。

He pushed the door open and walked out into the wind.
