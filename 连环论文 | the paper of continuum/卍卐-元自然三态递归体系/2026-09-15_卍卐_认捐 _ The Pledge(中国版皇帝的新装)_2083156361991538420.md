认捐 / The Pledge

一

新单位挂牌那天，主任说：新单位，新气象，不搞旧规矩。

The new office opened on a Tuesday. The director said: New unit, new spirit, no old rules.

第一次捐款，群里没人说话。大家都等着。

The first donation drive, no one spoke in the chat. Everyone waited.

办公室主任老赵先在群里发了一个红包，备注：“起个头。”

Office manager Zhao sent a red envelope in the chat first, with a note: "Just to start things off."

底下秒接。科员五十，副科长八十，科长一百，副局长一百五，局长二百。

Replies came instantly. Clerk fifty, deputy section chief eighty, section chief a hundred, deputy bureau chief a hundred and fifty, bureau chief two hundred.

没有人商量过。但数字整整齐齐。

No one had discussed it. But the numbers lined up perfectly.

老赵在群里说：感谢各位同事的爱心，本次捐款全部上交。

Zhao posted in the chat: Thank you all for your generosity. All donations will be submitted upward.

大家回复：应该的。

Everyone replied: Our duty.

二

第二次捐款，文件下来了。红头，两页纸，最后一页是认捐表。

The second drive, an official document came down. Red letterhead, two pages, the last page was a pledge form.

老赵在群里发了一条消息：按上次标准。

Zhao posted a message in the chat: Same standard as last time.

底下秒回：收到。

Replies came instantly: Understood.

没有人问“上次标准是多少”。每个人都记得自己上次捐了多少。

No one asked what the standard was. Everyone remembered exactly what they gave last time.

三

第三次，文件没下来。但老赵在群里发了一条消息：今年的捐款，参照去年标准。

The third time, no document came down. But Zhao posted in the chat: This year's donations, refer to last year's standard.

底下秒回：收到。

Replies came instantly: Understood.

有人私下问老赵：今年还涨不涨？

Someone privately asked Zhao: Are we raising it this year?

老赵回：不用涨，别搞特殊。

Zhao replied: No need to raise. Don't make yourself stand out.

那人放心了。

That person felt relieved.

四

今年来了个新人，小林，刚毕业。

This year, a new hire arrived. Lin, a fresh graduate.

第一次捐款，他在群里发了三百。

His first donation drive. He posted three hundred in the chat.

群里安静了。

The chat went silent.

那三秒钟的安静，比任何一句话都响。

Those three seconds of silence were louder than any words.

老赵私信他：“你刚来，按规矩来就行。科员五十。”

Zhao messaged him privately: "You just started. Follow the rules. Clerk gives fifty."

小林撤回三百，重发五十。

Lin retracted the three hundred and resent fifty.

群里恢复了正常。

The chat returned to normal.

有人在小林背后说了一句：“新来的不懂事。”

Someone behind Lin's back muttered: "Newcomer doesn't know the ropes."

另一个人说：“没事，待两年就懂了。”

Another said: "It's fine. Give him two years, he'll learn."

五

两年后，小林成了老人。又来一个新人，捐了三百。

Two years later, Lin was the old hand. Another new hire arrived. He gave three hundred.

小林盯着屏幕看了很久。

Lin stared at the screen for a long time.

他想起自己第一年的三百块钱，想起那三秒钟的安静，想起老赵的私信，想起自己撤回重发时手指有点抖。

He remembered his own three hundred from his first year. The three seconds of silence. Zhao's private message. His fingers trembling slightly as he retracted and resent.

他现在是老人了。他知道该怎么做。

He was the old hand now. He knew what to do.

他私信新人：“你刚来，按规矩来就行。科员五十。”

He messaged the new hire: "You just started. Follow the rules. Clerk gives fifty."

新人撤回三百，重发五十。

The new hire retracted the three hundred and resent fifty.

群里没人注意到这件事。

No one in the chat noticed.

六

年底，办公室参与率百分之百。局里发了通报。主任在大会上表扬了大家：咱们单位，从来都是全员参与，一个不漏。

At year-end, the office participation rate hit one hundred percent. The bureau issued a notice. The director praised everyone at the meeting: Our unit has always had full participation, not a single person missing.

散会后，小林路过公示栏。

After the meeting, Lin passed by the bulletin board.

红榜上印着：本次捐款共计XX元，参与率100%。

The red sheet printed: Total donations XX yuan, participation rate 100%.

下面附了一张表，没有名字，没有金额，只有总数。

Below it was a table with no names, no individual amounts, only the total.

但每个人都记得自己捐了多少。

But everyone remembered exactly what they gave.

小林站了一会儿。

Lin stood there for a moment.

他想起自己第一年看到这张红榜的时候，问过自己一个问题：如果没有人规定标准，我会捐多少？

He remembered seeing this red sheet his first year and asking himself a question: If no one set a standard, how much would I give?

他现在已经不记得那个答案了。

He could no longer remember the answer.

他推开门，走进风里。

He pushed the door open and walked out into the wind.
