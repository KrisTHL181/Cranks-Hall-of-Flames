对任意坐标变换x^\mu\to x'^\mu，设变换雅可比行列式为J=\det(\partial x'/\partial x)。

第一，普通δ函数满足变换关系：\delta(f(x))=|J|\delta(f'(x'))；

第二，法向量模长项满足逆变变换：\sqrt{|g^{\mu\nu}\partial_\mu f\partial_\nu f|}=|J|^{-1}\sqrt{|g'^{\mu\nu}\partial'_\mu f'\partial'_\nu f'|}；

\delta_\Sigma(x)=|J|\cdot|J|^{-1}\delta_\Sigma'(x')=\delta_\Sigma'(x')\tag{3.3}
